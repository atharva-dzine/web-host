Process

How a film gets made

The same five steps on every project, so you always know what you're approving next.

  1. Brief & script

    We agree on the story, the message and where the film will run.

  2. You approve

    Storyboard

    Quick panels show framing, blocking and camera for every shot before production starts.

  3. Character & product locks

    Faces, products and locations are built as references so they match in every shot.

  4. Shot generation

    Each shot starts from a locked frame and is refined until the motion feels right.

  5. Edit, sound & finish

    Cut and finished with VO, music and sound design. Supers and packshots are added in post.
